Biggest Pain Points

The onboarding tour shows the wrong things at the wrong time — Advanced features fire before Alex has completed anything basic. The product is teaching before Alex is ready to learn. High severity · Research
Teammate adoption is a blocker Alex can't control — The tool only delivers its core value promise if the whole team switches. Alex's conversion depends on other people's behavior. High severity · Stated
The upgrade prompt appears too early — Alex needs adoption proof to get budget approval. The paywall fires before that proof exists, creating a catch-22. High severity · Stated

Top Opportunities

Replace the feature tour with one guided real task — A role-appropriate template pre-loaded and a "complete this in 5 minutes" prompt gets Alex to first value in the same session they signed up. This directly fixes the onboarding drop-off.
Tie the upgrade trigger to team adoption, not trial day count — Prompt the upgrade when 3+ teammates are active. This aligns the paywall with the moment Alex has proof of value — and the best chance of getting budget approved.

Recommended Focus

The critical drop-off is at onboarding. Alex arrives curious (+3 at sign-up) and leaves disoriented (−2 after the tour). The gap between arrival sentiment and first-session abandonment is where most trials are lost.

Fixing the onboarding stage — replacing the feature tour with a single guided workflow, pre-loading a role-matched template, and triggering the teammate invite at the moment of first task completion — addresses the core problem without requiring any architectural changes.
